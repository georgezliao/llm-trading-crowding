# Pre-registered impact x leverage x drawdown grid, Amendment 1 rerun

This report describes synthetic output from the uncalibrated HerdLab simulator. It is not evidence about real market behavior. This is the Amendment 1 rerun specified in `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`; the grid, seeds, episode count, eligibility rule, and selection rule are unchanged from the original run.

## Design

- Base config: `configs/baseline.yaml`
- Model version: Amendment 1 adds the pre-specified 20-step post-liquidation cooldown, peak-equity reset on resumption, and nonzero-quantity condition for forced-order labels.
- Grid: 5 x 3 x 3 = 45 cells; 10 episodes per cell; shared seeds `0-9` (450 total episodes)
- Impact sensitivities: `0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1`
- Leverage multipliers: `1, 5, 20`; each multiplier scales both baseline `q_max` and `order_limit`
- Drawdown limits: `0.05, 0.1, 0.2`
- Parallel workers: 10
- Unwind horizon: 10 steps. The step-1 diagnostic threshold is the pooled score quantile 0.95, with fitted value `0.04274896274`; this map makes no held-out prediction claim.
- Each diagnostic value is the unweighted mean of episode-level values, except maximum agents forced (cell maximum), median max drawdown (episode median), and minimum equity (minimum over every agent-time value).

## Pre-registered eligibility and selection

A cell is eligible only when `max_drawdown = 0.20`, at least 5/10 episodes have nonzero forced flow, mean forced-step fraction is at most 0.10, median price max drawdown is at most 0.30, and every agent-equity value is finite and nonnegative. Selection takes the lowest impact sensitivity, breaking ties by the lowest leverage multiplier.
